# Competitive Opportunity Map

## Positioning Space

MinkinSpace should avoid presenting itself as only:
- a generic AI agency;
- a classic SEO agency with a GEO add-on;
- an automation vendor;
- a marketing-only consultancy.

Preferred territory:
- practical AI and automation consulting for business operations;
- clear bridge between workflow improvement, technical readiness, analytics, marketing, and AI-search visibility;
- implementation-minded diagnosis without unsupported claims.

## Messaging Principles

- Lead with business problems and decisions, not tools.
- Explain how services connect into a coherent operating system for growth and efficiency.
- Use concrete service outcomes only when approved.
- Keep missing proof points as `TODO_CONTENT`.

## Homepage Hypothesis

A homepage structured around "diagnose, prioritize, implement, improve" should be more credible than one focused on broad AI transformation language.
