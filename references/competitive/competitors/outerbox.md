Source: https://www.outerboxdesign.com/digital-marketing-services/seo/generative-engine-optimization/
Access date: 2026-08-15

## Observed Positioning

- GEO positioned as part of a broader search program.
- Focuses on access, direct answers, proof near claims, entity clarity, and conversion paths.
- Warns against treating GEO as a bolt-on tactic.

## Page And Offer Pattern

- Educational page with tactical categories.
- Integrates SEO, content, prompt testing, entity work, analytics, and conversion.

## Implications For MinkinSpace

- GEO page should connect AI visibility to SEO, website structure, content evidence, and conversion.
- Structured data must match visible page content and avoid unsupported claims.
