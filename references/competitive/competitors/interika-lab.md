# Competitor Note: Interika Lab

Source: https://interikalab.com/
Access date: 2026-08-15

## Observed Positioning

- AI business automation with ROI calculated before project start.
- Leads with diagnosis, pilot/MVP, integrations, and measurable criteria.
- Service areas include HR, back office, analytics/assistants, custom AI, RAG, agents, local/on-prem contours, and security.
- Uses a loss/ROI calculator and detailed process stages.

## Page And Offer Pattern

- Free diagnosis CTA.
- Strong problem-first structure: routine, lost requests, expensive manual work.
- Implementation journey is explicit from diagnosis to pilot, process integration, support, and development.

## Implications For MinkinSpace

- This is a close positioning competitor for AI automation consulting.
- MinkinSpace should emphasize cross-domain breadth beyond AI automation alone: IT readiness, marketing analytics, social marketing, and GEO.
- The homepage and service pages should keep the "diagnose, prioritize, implement, measure" structure visible.
