# Competitor Note: ProcessTech

Source: https://processtech.ru/
Access date: 2026-08-15

## Observed Positioning

- Business automation based on the RunaWFE configurable software platform.
- Targets enterprises, SMBs, individual entrepreneurs, and specialized process needs.
- Emphasizes customizable and affordable process automation, with cloud and local pricing.
- Process flow includes data collection, process modeling/implementation, integration, support, and development.

## Page And Offer Pattern

- Consultation/demo CTA.
- Platform-led BPM positioning.
- Transparent entry pricing and industry/use-case sections.

## Implications For MinkinSpace

- MinkinSpace should distinguish itself from platform-led BPM vendors by staying technology-agnostic and consulting-led.
- Business Process Automation messaging should lead with process audit, fit, roadmap, and integration options.
- If platform recommendations are included later, they should be framed as selected after diagnosis, not as a fixed platform.
