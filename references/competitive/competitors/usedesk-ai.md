# Competitor Note: UseDesk AI

Source: https://usedeskai.ru/
Access date: 2026-08-15

## Observed Positioning

- AI support agent focused on closing typical customer-service requests across text channels.
- Strong ROI and automation framing, including calculator-style economics.
- Uses customer-service cases by segment and channel.
- Emphasizes CRM, ERP, helpdesk, messenger, and email integrations.

## Page And Offer Pattern

- Demo CTA and ROI calculator.
- Quantified outcomes and pay-for-result pricing narrative.
- Strong operational proof structure around gradual automation, knowledge-base quality, and integrations.

## Implications For MinkinSpace

- MinkinSpace can borrow the pattern of explaining measurable process economics, but must not invent savings or automation percentages.
