# Competitor Note: OlineAI

Source: https://oline.ai/
Access date: 2026-08-15

## Observed Positioning

- Voice and text AI assistants for customer communication.
- Productized offers include phone assistants, website chat widgets, messengers, HR assistant, provider support, retail, and education scenarios.
- The page uses visible entry prices and industry-specific use cases.
- Emphasizes fast launch, turnkey delivery, integration, security, and 152-FZ alignment.

## Page And Offer Pattern

- Call/order CTA.
- Product-card structure with prices.
- Industry tabs and use-case lists help visitors self-identify.
