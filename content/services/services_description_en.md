## 2. AI Sales & Customer Service

### Block 1. AI for sales and customer service

We help process inbound leads faster, reduce first response time, and avoid losing potential customers. AI takes over standard communication stages: clarifies the need, answers standard questions, qualifies the request, records data in the CRM, and hands prepared context to the manager.

Our solutions can work with a website, messengers, email, CRM, and other services.

What the business gets:

- faster first response to the customer;
- 24/7 initial communication;
- more processed requests without increasing workload;
- a unified lead qualification standard;
- a transparent funnel and preserved communication context.

**CTA: Get a consultation**

### Block 2. AI works together with your team

Request received -> Need clarification -> Qualification -> CRM -> Manager handoff -> Result analysis

AI automates routine and repeatable stages, while complex, disputed, and commercially important situations are handed over to an employee. Before launch, we define metrics: response speed, conversion into a qualified lead, share of lost requests, and managers' time spent processing requests. Our goal is not to replace the sales department, but to increase its throughput and quality of work.
