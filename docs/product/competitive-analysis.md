# Competitive Analysis

Status: preliminary research draft
Access date: 2026-08-15

## Summary

Competitors in AI consulting, automation, and GEO commonly sell clarity in a noisy AI market. The strongest pages make the service concrete through audits, roadmaps, prompt/query analysis, process mapping, structured content, entity authority, and monitoring. MinkinSpace Consulting should differentiate as a pragmatic partner that connects AI, automation, IT readiness, marketing analytics, and AI-search visibility into one practical consulting path.

## Competitor Set

| Competitor | Relevant Area | Key Pattern | MinkinSpace Implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intero Digital | GEO / digital marketing | Entity authority, content ecosystem, AI visibility audit, roadmap, implementation | Do not compete on scale claims without proof; emphasize practical consulting integration. |
| Potenture | AI Search / GEO | Mentions, citations, positioning accuracy, prompt sets, entity gaps | Make AI-search service concrete and measurable without promising rankings. |
| Brand75 | Small-business AI consulting | Audit and roadmap before implementation, operational use cases | Package AI Pre-Sales and automation around practical workflow improvements. |
| Axiant | Business process automation | Process-first automation and governance | Lead automation messaging with diagnosis before tools. |
| OuterBox | GEO / SEO | GEO as part of search, content, entity, analytics, and conversion | Connect SEO/GEO to visible content, structured data, and conversion paths. |
| Qognition | GEO / AI marketing | Broad capability taxonomy and pipeline framing | Use focused MVP service structure with strong internal links. |

## Website Pattern Findings

- Most high-converting competitors use a consultation, audit, assessment, or strategy-session CTA.
- GEO pages explain the shift from search results to generated answers, then offer a diagnostic and roadmap.
- Practical AI consulting pages avoid generic AI language and instead describe operational pain points.
- Credibility is often built through proof, frameworks, named methodologies, or case results; MinkinSpace should use only approved evidence.
- Service overview pages should make adjacent services easy to compare and route visitors to the right next step.

## Risks

- A generic "AI transformation" message will blend into the market.
- Unsupported proof claims would violate the project rules and weaken trust.
- A design that looks too futuristic or dashboard-heavy conflicts with the approved design direction.
- GEO content must avoid guaranteeing AI-answer inclusion or ranking.

## Recommended Direction

Position MinkinSpace Consulting around practical improvement:

> MinkinSpace Consulting helps teams identify, prioritize, and implement practical improvements across AI, automation, IT infrastructure, analytics, marketing, and AI-search visibility.

This is a working positioning line and requires owner approval before use as public copy.
