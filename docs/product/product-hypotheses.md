# Product Hypotheses

Status: draft

## Primary Hypotheses

1. Visitors will respond better to a practical "diagnose and prioritize" consultation offer than to broad AI transformation messaging.
2. Service pages that explain problems solved, process, deliverables, and next step will convert better than pages focused on tool names.
3. Bundling AI Search / GEO with marketing analysis and website content structure will feel more credible than selling GEO as an isolated tactic.
4. AI Pre-Sales and Business Process Automation should be framed around lead response, intake quality, follow-up, workflow handoffs, and reporting.
5. The site should support both executive buyers and operator buyers by pairing strategic outcomes with implementation details.

## Audience Hypotheses

| Audience | Likely Need | Site Response |
|---|---|---|
| Founder / owner | Understand where AI or automation can improve the business | Clear consultation CTA and service comparison |
| Marketing lead | Improve analytics, campaigns, and AI-search visibility | Marketing AI Analysis, Social Marketing, and AI Search / GEO cross-links |
| Sales / operations lead | Improve lead handling and internal workflows | AI Pre-Sales and Business Process Automation pages |
| Technical decision-maker | Validate infrastructure and implementation feasibility | IT Infrastructure Audit page and technical process notes |

## Offer Hypotheses

- Entry offer: consultation or diagnostic call.
- Follow-up offer: audit and implementation roadmap.
- Implementation offer: scoped service project.
