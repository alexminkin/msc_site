# Product Hypotheses

Status: draft

## Primary Hypotheses

1. Visitors will respond better to a practical "diagnose and prioritize" consultation offer than to broad AI transformation messaging.
2. Landing-page service cards that explain problems and next steps will convert better than tool-name-driven messaging.
3. Bundling AI Search / GEO with marketing analysis and website content structure will feel more credible than selling GEO as an isolated tactic.
4. AI Pre-Sales and Business Process Automation should be framed around lead response, intake quality, follow-up, workflow handoffs, and reporting.
5. The landing page should support both executive buyers and operator buyers by pairing strategic outcomes with implementation detail.

## Audience Hypotheses

| Audience | Likely Need | Landing Response |
|---|---|---|
| Founder / owner | Understand where AI or automation can improve the business | Clear consultation CTA and service comparison |
| Marketing lead | Improve analytics, campaigns, and AI-search visibility | Marketing AI Analysis, Social Marketing, and AI Search / GEO cards |
| Sales / operations lead | Improve lead handling and internal workflows | AI Pre-Sales and Business Process Automation cards |
| Technical decision-maker | Validate infrastructure and implementation feasibility | IT Infrastructure Audit card and approach section |

## Offer Hypotheses

- Entry offer: consultation or diagnostic call.
- Follow-up offer: audit and implementation roadmap.
- Implementation offer: scoped service project.

- Primary conversion: consultation request.
